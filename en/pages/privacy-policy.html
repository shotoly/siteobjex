3. Data Recipients and Sub-processors

Your personal data is never sold, leased, or rented to third parties for marketing purposes.

Data is handled exclusively by Robin Jallade and transmitted through secure technical sub-processors essential to our operations:

  • Web Hosting: Render Services, Inc. (San Francisco, USA), with production servers situated within the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) applied.
  • Workflow Automation: n8n (secure webhook relays).
  • Manufacturing & Order Tracking: Notion Labs, Inc. (SOC 2 Type II certified private workspace, GDPR DPA compliant).
  • Instant Production Alert: Telegram Messenger (encrypted private alert bot to ensure immediate production turn-around).
  • Media CDN: Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare R2 storage).

4. Cookie & Tracker Policy

Zero tracking policy: Objex3D does not run marketing pixels, behavioral analytics, or third-party advertising cookies.

The sole variable stored locally on your device is a key named theme in your browser's localStorage, used solely to remember your dark/light theme setting.

Under French CNIL guidelines and European ePrivacy standards, strictly necessary functional local settings are exempt from prior consent banners.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR

Pursuant to GDPR Articles 15 to 22, you hold the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): Request a copy of all personal data held about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Ask to correct inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17): Request deletion of your data from our records.
  • Right to Restriction (Art. 18): Request temporary limitation of data processing.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): Object at any time to data processing based on our legitimate interest.
